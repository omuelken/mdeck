# Less on the slide

Working memory holds only a few things at once, so a slide that makes people search wastes it [@sweller1988].

Words spoken alongside a picture beat the same words printed on it [@mayer2009, chap. 11].

# Ask, don't tell

As @roediger2006 showed, recalling something fixes it better than reading it again.

Lectures with active learning cut failure rates by about a third [@freeman2014; see also @roediger2006].

:::notes
Freeman et al. pooled 225 studies [-@freeman2014].
