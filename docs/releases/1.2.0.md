## Launch page

`mdeck dev` opens a launch page with a preview of the deck, links to the presenter, audience and reader views and the editor, and buttons to build, send and print it.

## Audience polls

`<poll room=… options=…>` shows live results and a QR code, and people vote on their phones. For hosted decks, run a room server with `mdeck live`.

## Language and components

- `lang` sets the deck's language, and mdeck's own words follow in German or English.
- Decks can share components from other folders with the `components:` setting.
