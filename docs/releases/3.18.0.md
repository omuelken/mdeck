A slide can now stay in the deck without being shown: `hidden: true` in its settings, or the eye beside it in the editor's slide list. The deck, the presenter view, the audience window, the reader and PDFs skip it, and the page numbers count without it; the editor still shows it, greyed out. Built files, files from `mdeck send` and PDFs keep nothing of a hidden slide: not its words, pictures, drawings or answers. Thanks to Oliver Mülken ([@omuelken](https://github.com/omuelken)) for the feature.
