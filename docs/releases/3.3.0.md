## Citations

Set `bibliography: refs.bib` and cite as `[@smith2020, p. 12]`. Each slide lists the works it cites, `<bibliography />` lists them all, and `csl:` picks the style (APA by default).

## See how slides look

`mdeck check --render` builds the deck in Chrome and reports content that does not fit or is hard to read. `mdeck snapshot` saves slides as PNG files.

## Follow along

`?view=follow` lets people follow the talk on their phone or laptop, step by step, and page back. `<qrcode follow />` shows its link on a slide.

## Polls

- An eye button shows or hides a poll's results; `results="hidden"` starts hidden.
- `--no-polls` leaves poll slides out of `mdeck send`, `mdeck build --reader` and `mdeck pdf`.
