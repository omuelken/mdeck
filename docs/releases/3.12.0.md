## A new presenter view

One toolbar of icons under the slide, and **This slide** for what the slide offers: a poll's lock, results and right answer, a video's play, the numbered parts to zoom into. Who is connected and the settings sit behind two icons. **?** lists the keys that work right now.

## Presenting

- A phone becomes a clicker: notes, time, big buttons and the slide's controls. Pair it, or an iPad, from the presenter view.
- **B** blacks out the screen, **O** shows all slides, **J** puts the join code over any slide.
- Questions no longer carry their own QR code, and phones can send reactions 👍 🐢.
- With `MDECK_SERVER` and its key set, `mdeck run` uses your server by itself.
