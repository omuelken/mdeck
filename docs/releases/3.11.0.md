## Time your talk

Give slides a `time: 2m`. The presenter view counts down each slide and shows whether you are ahead or behind. `mdeck rehearse` times the slides for you and saves the times into the deck.

## Presenting

- Drawing and the laser from a paired iPad arrive whole and sooner; the laser stays while the pen is down.
- Type a slide number in the presenter view to jump there.
- `--slide 3,5-7` sends, builds or prints only part of a deck.
- The tour now comes in English, German and Arabic.

## For authors

- Palette-coloured SVG drawings build up step by step (`data-step="1"`, `"2"` …), and `mdeck check` reviews them.
- Components can share state across slides and windows: `useShared` from `mdeck/component`.
