mdeck 3.11 is about the talk itself: how long it takes, which language it is given in, and presenting it from an iPad.

## Time your talk

Give a slide `time: 2m` and the deck `time: 20m`; slides without a time share what is left. While you present, the presenter view counts down the time left on the slide and tells you whether you are ahead of the plan or behind it.

To find the times, rehearse: `mdeck rehearse`, or **Rehearse** in the presenter view and on the launch page. mdeck times each slide as you go. At the end, a table offers each slide's new time, rounded to 15 seconds, for you to adjust, and **Save to deck** writes them into the slides' settings without reloading the open windows. The editor has a Time field for each slide and a Length for the deck.

The timer now reads the clock instead of counting seconds, so it no longer falls behind, and it carries on through a reload. **T** starts and pauses it.

## One talk, several languages

mdeck's own words now come in Arabic too, next to English and German: with `lang: ar`, or a file named `talk.ar.md`, the reader view, the control bar, polls, the phones' answer page and callout titles are Arabic. Slides set from right to left keep palette-coloured drawings and Latin-script citations, such as (Roediger & Karpicke, 2006), in their own direction.

The tour shows it: it comes in English, German and Arabic (`examples/showcase/slides.en.md`, `.de.md`, `.ar.md`), and `mdeck run slides.en.md` switches between them with **L**. It now also shows citations and a word cloud for the room.

## Presenting from an iPad

- **Drawing arrives whole.** A stroke or laser trail drawn on a paired iPad could stop short in the audience window when the network dropped or reordered a piece of it. Pieces are now sent one after another, a lost one is sent again, and a piece that arrives early waits for the one before it.
- **Drawing arrives sooner**: about 60 ms after the pen, instead of 100 to 150 ms.
- **The laser stays while the pen is down**, in every window, so you can circle or underline something while you talk about it.
- **Jump to a slide by its number**: the slide number in the presenter view is a field. Type a number and press Enter, or Go on the iPad.
- **Faster slide changes in large decks with polls.** The presenter view no longer reads the whole deck again on every change.
- **YouTube videos play.** On a device that opened the deck through a server, `<videoplayer>` showed "This video is unavailable".

## Drawings that build up

A drawing that uses the palette (an SVG with `var(--…)`) builds up in the order its parts are numbered: `data-step="1"`, `data-step="2"` … appear one press at a time, parts with the same number together. With hotspots on, `data-hotspot` on a part makes it a place to zoom to once it is shown. A drawing used on two slides keeps its arrowheads, gradients and `<use>` copies on both.

`mdeck check` now looks at the drawings too: palette colours without a fallback, fixed colours that ignore the palette and dark mode, styles that leak into the page, and steps or hotspots in a drawing that is shown as a plain picture. The slide-writing skill draws diagrams this way and checks them in light and dark.

## Share part of a deck

`--slide 3,5-7` for `mdeck send`, `mdeck build` and `mdeck pdf` keeps only these slides, with their drawings: the part of a course given in one lecture, for example. A shared part keeps the time its slides were planned to take, so the countdown fits.

## For component authors

Components can share state across slides and windows:

```js
import { useShared, useView } from 'mdeck/component'
const [count, setCount] = useShared('count', 0)
```

`useShared` works like `useState`, but every slide, the presenter's slide, the audience window, a paired iPad and windows opened later see the same value. `useView()` says which window a component is in. `mdeck/component` is the first part of mdeck that components may rely on; the authoring reference says what it promises.

## Also fixed

- On a slide with several areas (`:::slot`), a code block in one area turned the next area into code. Citations and footnotes now leave code alone.
- A `<style>` on a slide left out of a shared deck (`--slide`, `--no-polls`) now moves to the first slide kept, since it styles the whole deck.
- The note that slides with polls were left out appears only when a chosen slide had a poll.
- Under `mdeck run`, the presenter view has a button back to the launch page.
