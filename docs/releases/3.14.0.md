## Presenting from an iPad

- Neither bar covers the slide in the presenter view: the slide sits below the bar and above the open drawing toolbar, as large as fits, and takes the toolbar's strip back while it is folded. Both bars are a little smaller there.
- The drawing toolbar starts open.
- Without full screen, the view fits the part of the window Safari shows.
- A bin beside a selection of strokes deletes it, without opening the toolbar.
- An iPad that is no longer paired, after a restart of `mdeck run` or **Unpair**, says so instead of keeping its slide changes, drawings and polls to itself.
- Pairing codes and join links use the laptop's address of now, after it moved to another network, and say when no other device can reach it.

## Command line

`mdeck --help` lists the commands, one line each; `mdeck <command> --help` shows a command's options.

## Launch page

Answers and drawings are dropped at once, without asking and without a backup.

## Fixed

- After a restart of `mdeck run`, a presenter view opened or reloaded no longer takes the projector back to the first slide.
- Since 3.13.0, opening or reloading the presenter view on an iPad took the laptop back to the first slide when nobody had changed slides for 15 s.
- Two quick language switches (L, L) could leave a slide in the language before.
- The join code shown with **J** on a paired iPad now shows in the audience window and on phones following the talk.
- Two things circled with the laser one after the other are no longer joined by a line.

Update your poll server too for the 15 s fix when you present through it.
