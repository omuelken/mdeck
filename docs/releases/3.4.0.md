## Polls for peer instruction

- A lock closes an activity: vote, close, show the bars, then show the answer.
- Showing the right answer tells each phone whether its vote was right.
- `multiple` allows several picks, and polls count "12 of 31 answered".

## New questions and maths

`<numeric>` lets phones type a number, with `answer` and `tolerance`. Questions and options may use Markdown and `$…$` maths.

## Results kept

Answers are saved beside the deck in `<slides>.results.json` while `mdeck run` runs. Builds, `mdeck send` and `mdeck pdf` show them (`--no-results` leaves them out), and `mdeck results` prints them as CSV.

## Themes

Links take the palette's accent, quotations have their own style, and lists inside lists are spaced and numbered properly, in every theme.
