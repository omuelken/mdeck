## Draw on your slides

Press `D` to draw with pen, highlighter and eraser, in the deck or the presenter view. `mdeck dev` saves drawings in `<deck>.ink.json`, and the audience window shows them as they are drawn.

## Present from an iPad

With `mdeck dev --host`, the launch page shows a QR code that pairs an iPad as the presenter view while the laptop drives the projector. Touch screens get a presenter layout where the slide fills the screen.

## More audience questions

`<scale>`, `<wordcloud>` and `<question>`, and `<qrcode join />` for a slide where people join. A room server started with `mdeck live` needs 1.4 to pass drawings on.
