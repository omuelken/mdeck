## Logos

- `logo-dark: ./img/logo-white.png` in `meta` gives a deck a second logo for dark slides, shown as it is instead of the inverted `logo`.
- With `show.organization: all` the logo appears on every slide, in the header's line; by default it stays on the title slide.

## Presenting

- The presenter view's keys (T, H, L, N, F, Escape) work after a click on the slide too; Home goes to the first slide and End to the last.
- **I** (hide drawings) reaches every screen, as B does. Drawing starts with the pen on a computer and with the laser pointer on a touch screen; the highlighter brightens the text under it on dark slides.
- A key or a click in a frozen audience window lets it follow again, should the presenter view have closed.
- On a phone, the clicker's top bar fits again; freeze (Z) is in the settings menu's "During the talk" row there. The **?** button is in the iPad layout too.
- With a phone clicker paired, the presenter view no longer warns "Phones follow another screen" while both show the same slide.
- A presenter button switched on and off again no longer keeps a light frame.
- After a reload of the presenter view, the audience window follows it again instead of going back to the first slide.

## Polls and phones

- In a lecture hall behind one network address, phones are limited one by one; before, most answers after the first thirty in ten seconds were refused.
- **Reset** on a poll reaches the phones, and a poll server that restarts keeps the answers of the poll on screen.
- `mdeck run --server` keeps trying while the web server in front of the room server restarts, and notices a connection that died while the laptop slept.
- "12 of 31 answered" counts the phones that can answer; the answer page reads right to left for Arabic, Farsi and Hebrew decks and keeps typed text while a poll is locked.

## Drawings and the editor

- Undoing a slide's first drawing is saved; a drawing that could not be saved says so; Ctrl+C right after a stroke or an answer no longer loses it.
- Drawings show in every language of a multilingual deck.
- The editor keeps the last change when its tab is closed, shows changes saved in another tab, retries failed saves with a growing pause and **Try again**, and survives the slide file being moved away and back.

## Themes and charts

- Third- and fourth-level headings and rules are set in the theme's type in every theme, and code in split columns is smaller in every theme.
- Line charts with dates put their ticks on years, quarters, months or days; a bar chart's `max` ends its axis there; chart labels land on the right side on right-to-left slides.

## Command line

- `mdeck migrate` is gone: it updated decks from before 2.0. A deck or extension that still uses the old names is told what to change.
- `mdeck snapshot` takes `--no-drawings` and `--no-results`; `mdeck build -o dist/` writes `dist/index.html`; a deck's `time:` may be `1h30`.
- Pictures in `poster`, `srcset` and `url(…)` are checked, copied and embedded; `mdeck check` explains more, and errors that showed a stack trace say what is wrong.

Update your poll server too when you present through it: the phone limits, poll resets, kept answers and the clicker warning need it.

The changelog lists every change.
