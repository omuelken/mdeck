## New themes `academic` and `sketch`

`academic` is for lectures: smaller type, a running head and statements set like theorems. `sketch` is a sketchbook with handwriting, sticky notes and taped-in photos. New palette `pastel`.

## Lecture callouts

`definition`, `theorem`, `lemma`, `corollary`, `proof`, `example` and `remark`, labelled in English or German.

## Pictures in the palette's colours

SVG pictures can use `var(--accent)` and the other palette colours, and follow the theme and light or dark.

## Sharing

- The reader view keeps the sender's theme and palette, with only a light/dark switch.
- Polls in a PDF or a sent file show their question and options, "Answered live during the talk".
